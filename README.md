## Candidates and limitations

1. JANVS premium 10-piece pack: https://janvs.it/products/polo-t-shirt-vintage-premium-lotto-da-10-30-50-pz
   - Best plausible fit, but thin margin. Supplier describes Grade A with up to 10% Grade B; brands may include Ralph Lauren, Lacoste, Tommy Hilfiger, Nike and Adidas, with no guaranteed ratios.
   - €12 average is conditional on a good mix of desirable polos. If the mix only supports €10 average, downside revenue is €64 and the loss is €14.90 before other costs.
   - Supplier states bulk sales are final and nonconformity should be reported within 48 hours with evidence: https://janvs.it/policies/refund-policy
   - Individual items have not been authenticated. Supplier originality claims alone are insufficient.
2. Denim Depot: https://denimdepotnederland.com/products/10x-branded-t-shirts-grade-a-b
   - Lower landed cost but worse condition risk: A/B mix with approximately 20% Grade C mentioned in the product description. This conflicts with the user's Grade A preference.
   - Mixed reviews; review allegations do not establish the condition of this specific batch: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/denimdepotnederland.com
3. Premium Vintage Wholesale: https://www.premiumvintagewholesale.com/en/products/jerseys-coogi-vintage-8-unidad
   - Legacy URL leads to a mixed clearance product. Live checkout accepted the 10-item €45 option despite inconsistent indexed stock text.
   - Supplier describes 80–100% Grade A, up to 20% B, and up to 20% unbranded/vintage items. Random categories; supplier states no returns/refunds. Too uncertain for a strong first purchase recommendation.
   - Mixed reviews: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.premiumvintagewholesale.com
4. JANVS mini pack: https://janvs.it/products/mini-pack-5-pezzi-vintage-misti
   - Rejected: shipping and purchase cost per sellable item are too high.

## Latest advice given

Consider JANVS only if the supplier provides photos of the actual batch and the visible brands, condition and styles justify approximately €12 average resale. Do not buy solely from representative product photos. If actual contents cannot be inspected, the alternative is individually chosen second-hand items within the €100 budget. That alternative has not yet been researched into a purchase list.

Washing means cleaning items if necessary after inspection and following care labels. Every item does not necessarily need washing. Laundry and packaging were excluded from gross profit.
